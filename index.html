About the Naturespace

Welcome to the virtual Naturespace. This 3D space is a virtual duplicate of the real Naturespace at ASU's Hayden Library in Tempe, Arizona.

This project is a collaboration with Naturespace and the Map and Geospatial Hub at ASU Library.



The Naturespace is a hands-on Natural History Collection at Hayden Library where ASU affiliates can find inspiration from nature through art, design, biomimicry and science.

This virtual space is an opportunity to view and provide an overview of Naturespace's collection digitally

Explore the Naturespace

Explore the space by clicking on a specimen or object to learn about it.



When you click a specimen, a pop-up box explains what it is and provides a link to learn more.
